Privacy Notice — Real Estate Agent Test

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This privacy notice describes the current personal test prototype. It is not a production company service.

Who operates this demo?

This is a personal software prototype operated by Muftahi. It is not an official service of a real-estate company. The property listings used in demonstrations are sample data and may not describe available properties.

Information processed

When you message the WhatsApp test number, the demo processes the text of your message, your WhatsApp sender identifier/phone number, message metadata, and the AI-generated reply. Please do not send sensitive, financial, health, identity, or other confidential information.

How the information is used and shared

  • Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform delivers messages to and from the demo.
  • The message text and recent conversation context are sent to Groq to generate a reply.
  • During local demonstrations, requests may pass through ngrok to the developer's computer.
  • The AI may search a local database containing sample property listings.

Meta, Groq, and ngrok process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Do not use this demo with real customer data.

Storage and retention

The demo keeps recent conversation context temporarily in the running application's memory. It does not save chat history to the property database. The developer console displays incoming message text and the generated reply during testing. Stopping the application clears its in-memory conversation context; console output may remain visible in the local development session until that session is closed. This prototype does not provide production-grade retention controls.

Deletion requests and contact

To ask the operator to delete information controlled by this demo, contact yboody634@gmail.com and include the WhatsApp number used in the test and a description of your request. The operator will remove any information retained by the demo where technically available. Information held by Meta, Groq, or ngrok may need to be requested from those providers under their respective processes.
